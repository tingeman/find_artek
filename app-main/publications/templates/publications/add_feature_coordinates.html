How to enter the point
  • One point per feature. x is always the easting or the longitude, y the northing or the latitude, also for longitude/latitude systems (EPSG:4326, EPSG:4747). Longitudes west of Greenwich are negative (Nuuk: x = -51.7, y = 64.2).
  • Reference system: any EPSG code; the list below the field has the common ones for Greenland. UTM zone by longitude: zone n covers (n - 31) x 6 to (n - 30) x 6 degrees, i.e. 18: 78-72 W, 19: 72-66 W, 20: 66-60 W, 21: 60-54 W, 22: 54-48 W (Nuuk, Ilulissat, Kangerlussuaq), 23: 48-42 W, 24: 42-36 W (Tasiilaq), 25: 36-30 W, 26: 30-24 W, 27: 24-18 W (Ittoqqortoormiit), 28: 18-12 W, 29: 12-6 W.
  • GR96 or WGS84? GR96 (EPSG:4747, GR96 / UTM EPSG:3178-3189) is the Greenland reference frame, fixed to the plate in 1996; WGS84 follows the global frame. They differ by about 1 m today, which matters only for precise (GNSS) positions. Choose the system your instrument or source used. The map copy (WGS84) uses PROJ's transformation without a time-dependent model (good to about 1 m); the coordinates as entered, the reference system and the GNSS observation date are stored with the feature for precise work.
  • Values that look wrong (outside Greenland, swapped values, a UTM easting outside the zone) are shown as warnings before anything is saved; you can correct them or save anyway.

  • If you leave x, y and the reference system unchanged, the stored geometry stays as it is. Features with more than one point, or with lines or polygons, are edited on the map.
