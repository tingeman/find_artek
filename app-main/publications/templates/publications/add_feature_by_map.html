How to draw
  • Use the tools in the top left corner of the map: marker for a point, line (click each vertex; click the last vertex again or press "Finish"), polygon (click each corner; click the first corner to close it). A feature may hold several points, lines and polygons, in any mix.
  • Edit layers (pencil): drag markers and vertices, then press "Save" in the toolbar. Delete layers (bin): click the shapes to remove, then "Save" ("Clear all" removes everything).
  • Polygons must not cross themselves, and polygons of one feature must not overlap.
  • The map is in the polar projection EPSG:3574: north is up for Greenland (elsewhere north points towards the North Pole, so the map looks turned). The drawing is stored in WGS84 (longitude/latitude) as "digitized on the map". To enter exact coordinates, use "Enter coordinates" on the report page.
